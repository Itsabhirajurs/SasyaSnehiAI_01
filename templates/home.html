What Sashyasnehi Does

Eight intelligent modules working together for complete crop health insight.

01

Disease Detection

MobileNetV2 transfer learning identifies crop diseases from leaf photos with high accuracy.

02

Severity Estimation

HSV colour analysis measures the infected area percentage and classifies it as Mild, Moderate, or Severe.

03

Weather Risk Scoring

Live OpenWeather data — humidity, temperature and rain probability — adjusts the overall disease risk score.

04

Chemical Safety

Cross-checks your past chemicals against a safety database and flags soil & health hazards with safer alternatives.

05

Smart Advisory

Rule-based engine generates contextual treatment steps, watering advice and soil-specific guidance.

06

Gemini AI Chat

Ask follow-up questions in your language. Google Gemini answers with farm-specific context in real time.

07

Nearby Help

For severe cases, find the nearest agricultural consultant or nursery on Google Maps instantly.

08

Multilingual

Advisory results translated to English, Hindi or Kannada — more languages coming soon.
