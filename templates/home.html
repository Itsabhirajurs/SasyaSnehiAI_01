💡 Quick Farming Tips

Science-backed practices for healthier crops.

🌡️

Spray in the Morning

Apply fungicides/insecticides early morning (6–9 AM) when wind is calm, temperatures are low, and drift is minimal.

💧

Drip Over Flood Irrigation

Drip irrigation saves 40–50% water, reduces humidity near plants and significantly decreases fungal disease incidence.

🔄

Crop Rotation Matters

Rotating tomato with non-solanaceous crops breaks pest cycles. Never plant tomato after tomato or potato consecutively.

🧪

Soil Testing Every Season

Test NPK, pH and micronutrients before each crop. Blind fertilizer use is the #1 cause of toxicity and yield losses.

🐛

Scout Weekly for Pests

Walk your fields every 7 days. Early detection of pest eggs on leaf undersides prevents 80% of chemical interventions.

🌿

Neem Oil as First Defense

Neem oil at 2–3 mL/L works against 200+ pest species, is safe for bees and humans, and resists resistance build-up.
